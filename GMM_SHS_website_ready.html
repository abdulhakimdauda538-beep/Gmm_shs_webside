About our school

Building knowledge, character and leadership

Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School is located at Beposo in the Ashanti Region of Ghana. The school community seeks to support learners to achieve academically, develop leadership skills and practise positive moral values.

This website is being prepared as the school's online information and learning platform. Official history, programmes, admissions information and contact details can be added after verification by the school administration.

School leadership

Welcome from the Headmaster

Umar Mudassir · Headmaster, Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School

Welcome to the official website of Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School (GMM SHS), Beposo.

Our school is committed to providing quality education in an environment that promotes academic excellence, discipline, moral values and personal development.

We believe that education goes beyond the classroom. It involves nurturing responsible, confident and productive individuals who can contribute positively to society.

Through the dedication of our teachers, staff, parents and stakeholders, we continue to work towards creating opportunities for every student to discover their potential and achieve their goals.

I invite students, parents, alumni and prospective students to explore our website and learn more about our school, academic programmes, admissions and activities.

Together, let us build a future founded on knowledge, humility and excellence.

Thank you for visiting GMM SHS.

— Umar Mudassir, Headmaster

Our beginnings

Our History and Founder's Legacy

Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School (GMM SHS) was established in 2008 at Beposo, Ghana.

Founder: Dr. Amin Bonsu

First Headmaster: Umar Mudassir

Current Headmaster: Umar Mudassir

Our Founder's Legacy — Dr. Amin Bonsu

Dr. Amin Bonsu is recognised as the founder of Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School. The school community honours his role in establishing an institution dedicated to education, moral development, knowledge and humility. This tribute is presented for the school's review and official confirmation.
